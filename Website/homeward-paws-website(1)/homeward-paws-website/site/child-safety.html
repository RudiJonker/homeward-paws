1. Our standard

Homeward Paws has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). Any such content is removed, the accounts involved are permanently banned, and we report it to the authorities.

Homeward Paws is an app for reuniting animals with their people. It is not a social network or a chat service, and accounts are for adults aged 18 and older. These standards apply to everything in the app and on this website, including reports, photos, profile details, sightings, claims and messages to support.

2. What is not allowed

  • Any content that sexualises, exploits or abuses a child, including images, drawings, text or links.
  • Grooming: using Homeward Paws to build contact with a child for abuse or exploitation, including using a lost-animal report as a pretext to meet or contact a child.
  • Sextortion, trafficking, or any attempt to arrange contact with a child for harmful purposes.
  • Sharing a child's personal information, such as a name, school or home address, in a report or claim.
  • Photos that show identifiable children where it is not necessary for the report.

3. How we prevent harm

  • Accounts are for adults only. Younger people may browse public reports but cannot post or contact others.
  • There is no open chat or private messaging between users. Contact happens through structured case responses, or by phone or WhatsApp only when a reporter chooses to show those details.
  • Every report, photo and profile can be reported from within the app.
  • Our moderators review reported content and can remove it and suspend accounts immediately.
  • Our safety guidance tells users to meet in public places and never to involve children in handovers with strangers.

4. How to report a concern

In the app

  1. Open the report, photo or profile.
  2. Tap Report and choose Child safety.
  3. Add any details that will help us, then submit. You do not need to leave the app.

By email

Email info@digitack.co.za with the subject line Child safety and a link to or description of the content. Do not attach or forward any abusive images: describe where they are instead.

If a child is in immediate danger, call SAPS on 10111. For support for a child, call Childline South Africa on 116 (free).

5. How we respond

  • We review child safety reports as our highest priority, aiming to act within 24 hours.
  • We remove CSAM and other violating content as soon as we become aware of it.
  • We permanently ban the accounts involved.
  • We preserve the information the law requires and report CSAM and child exploitation to the South African Police Service, as required by the Films and Publications Act, and to the Film and Publication Board. We cooperate with law-enforcement requests made through proper legal process.
  • We comply with all applicable child safety laws in South Africa and wherever Homeward Paws is available.
