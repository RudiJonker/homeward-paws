Three ways to help

Missing, found or just seen. Every report counts.

Each kind of report asks for the details that matter most, so the right people can act quickly.

Missing

My animal is missing

Register your animals once with photos and markings. If one goes missing, publish an alert with where and when it was last seen, and how people should approach it.

For animals you own or care for

Found

I found an animal

You have an animal safely with you, or it is with a vet, shelter or SPCA. The report shows a general area only. Where the animal is being kept stays private.

For animals that are secured

Sighting

I saw an animal

An animal is still roaming or has moved on. Log where and when you saw it and which way it was heading. It takes about a minute, and a photo is optional.

For animals that are still loose

How it works

From alert to reunion

1

Report

Publish a missing, found or sighting report with photos, a place and a time. Choose whether the public sees an exact spot or only the general area.

2

Share and alert

People nearby can follow the case, share it and add sightings. Homeward Paws suggests possible matches between missing and found reports.

3

Reunite

You check the details and arrange a safe handover. Once the reunion is confirmed, alerts stop and the case keeps its full history.

Privacy and safety

Built to protect owners, finders and animals

Missing-animal posts on social media often expose more than they should. Homeward Paws keeps the sensitive parts private by design.

Your home stays private

Your home address is never copied into a public report. You choose how precisely each incident location is shown.

Proof of ownership is never public

Microchip numbers, tag details and ownership evidence are stored privately and shared only with the person checking a claim.

You decide how people contact you

Your phone number and WhatsApp appear on a report only if you switch them on for that report.

People confirm every match

The app suggests possible matches and explains why. Only the people involved can confirm that an animal is theirs.

No public comment threads

Sightings, claims and updates use structured forms instead of open comments, so cases stay useful and free of speculation.

No money changes hands in the app

Homeward Paws never handles payments, rewards or donations. Anyone asking you to pay to get your animal back is a warning sign.

Made for South Africa

Local reports, organised the way you search

Reports are organised by province, city or town and suburb, so the people who can actually help see them first. Lists load before maps and photos are compressed, to keep data use low.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

When will Homeward Paws be available?

We are preparing the first release for Android on Google Play. This page will link to the Play Store as soon as the app is live.

Does it cost anything?

No. Homeward Paws is free. It shows a small number of static banner ads, which never appear on the screens you use to report an animal.

Is Homeward Paws an emergency or veterinary service?

No. If an animal is injured or in danger, contact a vet, the SPCA or your local animal-welfare organisation straight away. Report suspected theft to SAPS.

Does Homeward Paws decide who owns an animal?

No. The app helps people share information and evidence safely, but it does not decide legal ownership. The people involved, and where needed a vet, shelter or SAPS, confirm a reunion.

Can I offer a reward or collect donations?

Not through the app. Homeward Paws never handles money. Be careful of anyone who asks for payment before returning an animal.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Profile, then Delete account. You can also request deletion from this website without installing the app.
