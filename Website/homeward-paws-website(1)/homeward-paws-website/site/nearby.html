Animals near you

Missing animals reported on Homeward Paws in your province. If you think you have seen one, follow the owner's approach advice and tap Have you seen me? to tell the owner through the free app.

Your location is used only in your browser to pick your province. It is never sent to Homeward Paws or anyone else.

A free community app that helps reunite missing, found and sighted animals with their people in South Africa.
