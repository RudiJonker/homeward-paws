2. What Homeward Paws is

Homeward Paws is a free community platform for sharing structured information about missing, found and sighted animals in South Africa, so that animals can be reunited with their people.

Homeward Paws is a connection service only. It:

  • does not guarantee that any animal will be found or returned;
  • is not an emergency, rescue or veterinary service;
  • does not decide who legally owns an animal; and
  • does not handle payments, rewards or donations of any kind.

If an animal is injured or in danger, contact a vet, the SPCA or a local animal-welfare organisation. If you suspect theft, report it to SAPS.

5. Community rules

Do not use Homeward Paws to:

  • post false, misleading or duplicate reports, or claim an animal that is not yours;
  • ask for money, rewards or "transport fees" in exchange for returning an animal, or run any other scam;
  • sell, breed, rehome, adopt out or advertise animals, products or services;
  • harass, threaten, shame or speculate about owners, finders or anyone else;
  • publish someone else's private information, such as a home address or ID number;
  • post violent, graphic, sexual, hateful or illegal content, or any content that endangers children (see our child safety standards);
  • post photos in which people, especially children, can be identified when they do not need to be;
  • encourage anyone to trespass, chase animals into traffic, or put themselves in danger;
  • collect other users' information, use automated tools to scrape or flood the service, or interfere with its security.

6. Claims and reunions

  • Matching suggestions are only suggestions. Always check the details yourself.
  • If you are holding a found animal, ask a claimant for proof before handing it over, for example matching photos, a microchip scan at a vet or shelter, or features only the owner would know.
  • Ownership evidence you upload is seen only by the people reviewing your claim and, if needed, by us.
  • Homeward Paws does not verify ownership and is not responsible for a handover between users. If ownership is disputed, involve a vet, shelter, SPCA or SAPS.
  • Please mark a case as reunited or closed when it is resolved, so alerts stop.

7. Your safety

Meet in a public place or at a vet, shelter or police station, take someone with you, and never pay anyone to get an animal back. More guidance is on our help and safety page. You are responsible for your own safety when you meet other users or approach an animal.
